Baking a perfect loaf of sourdough bread requires patience and attention to environmental factors. The process starts with a healthy, active wild yeast starter culture. Mixing flour, water, salt, and the starter initiates a long, slow fermentation period. During this time, the dough must be gently folded at regular intervals to build a strong gluten framework. A properly proofed loaf is shaped and baked inside a very hot Dutch oven. The trapped steam inside the pot creates a beautifully blistered, crispy crust and an open interior crumb structure.

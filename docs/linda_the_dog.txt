Linda is an incredibly affectionate and energetic dog who brings constant joy to the household. She absolutely loves going on long walks around the neighborhood, sniffing every single blade of grass along the way. Her favorite activity is chasing tennis balls in the backyard, though she rarely wants to give them back once she catches them. When the day winds down, Linda transforms into a professional cuddle bug, curling up right next to her favorite people on the couch. Her expressive eyes and wagging tail make it impossible to stay in a bad mood around her.
